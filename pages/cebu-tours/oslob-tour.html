Quick Overview

Experience one of Cebu's most popular southern adventures with the Private Oslob Whale Shark Watching & Snorkeling Tour with Tumalog Falls. Swim alongside the gentle whale sharks in the clear waters of Oslob and enjoy an unforgettable snorkeling experience surrounded by marine life.

After the whale shark encounter, visit the beautiful Tumalog Falls, known for its refreshing misty cascades and relaxing natural scenery.

Tour Description & Itinerary

Start your journey with a scenic coastal drive to Oslob, followed by a short briefing on responsible interaction with the whale sharks. Then, hop on a boat and prepare for an awe-inspiring encounter as these massive yet friendly creatures gracefully swim beside you.

Sample Itinerary:

  • 03:00 AM – Pick up from hotel / resort
  • 05:30 AM – Arrival in Oslob & Light Breakfast
  • 06:30 AM – Swimming / Snorkeling with Whale Sharks
  • 07:30 AM – Cool down at Tumalog Falls
  • 09:00 AM – Lunch & Departure back to Cebu City
  • 01:00 PM – Arrival at Hotel Drop-off
