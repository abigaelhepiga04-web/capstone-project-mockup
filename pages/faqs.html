Booking & Payment

How much down payment do I need to confirm a booking?

A 50% deposit locks in your vehicle, guide, and tour date. The remaining balance is settled in cash on the day of the tour.

Do you accept credit or debit cards?

Yes. Cards carry a 5% service charge to cover processing fees. E-wallets and bank transfer are also available, each shown with their own fee (or none, for bank deposit) during checkout.

My card payment didn't go through. What are my options?

You can retry, or switch to GCash, Maya, PayPal, or a direct bank/InstaPay transfer from the same payment screen — no need to start the booking over.

Do you offer senior citizen or PWD discounts?

Yes. Senior citizen and children's discounts are calculated automatically in the booking form once you select the guest type — no promo code needed.

Tours & Pickup

Will my group share the vehicle with other travelers?

No. Every Uncle Ron's tour is private by default — the van is exclusively yours unless you specifically book a joiner/shared package.

Is hotel or airport pickup included?

Free pickup and drop-off is included for standard tours within Cebu City and Mactan. Pickups from farther towns may carry a small surcharge, shown before you confirm.

Can I customize an itinerary or add a side trip?

Absolutely. Most tour pages let you add optional side trips (like Osmeña Peak) directly in the booking form. For anything larger, message us and we'll build a custom plan.

Cancellations & Changes

Can I reschedule my tour date?

Yes, free of charge if you let us know at least 24 hours before your scheduled tour, subject to availability on the new date.

What happens if it rains on my tour day?

Weather-dependent tours (like island hopping or canyoneering) can be moved to another date at no extra cost. We'll coordinate with you the night before if conditions look questionable.
