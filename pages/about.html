OUR STORY

Local Guides, Genuine Cebu Hospitality

Uncle Ron's Travel & Tours started as a small family-run business helping visitors discover the best of Cebu and Bohol. What began with a single van and a handful of loyal guests has grown into a full-service, DOT-accredited travel agency.

Today, we still run things the same way we started: personally, honestly, and with a genuine love for showing people around our home. Every itinerary is built by locals who know these islands inside and out.
