OUR STORY Founded 2015

Local Guides, Genuine Visayan Hospitality

Uncle Ron's Travel & Tours started as a small family venture helping visitors discover the raw beauty of Cebu and Bohol. What began with a single van and a handful of eager travelers has evolved into a trusted, full-service travel agency.

Despite our growth, our core principle remains unchanged: to deliver personal, honest, and stress-free travel experiences. Every itinerary is crafted by Visayas locals who know the timing, hidden routes, and spots tourist maps miss.

  • Handpicked, time-optimized itineraries
  • Transparent pricing with no surprise add-ons
  • Professional, vetted local drivers & tour guides
