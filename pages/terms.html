Terms & Conditions

Last updated: January 2026

1

Bookings & Payment

A booking is confirmed once the required deposit (50% or full payment) is received. Prices are quoted in Philippine Pesos and may include a service charge depending on your chosen payment method.

2

Cancellations & Rescheduling

Rescheduling is free of charge with at least 24 hours' notice, subject to availability. Cancellations within 24 hours of the tour date may be non-refundable; please contact us directly to discuss your situation.

3

Guest Conduct & Safety

Guests are expected to follow guide instructions at all times, particularly during whale shark encounters and canyoneering activities, for their own safety and the protection of local wildlife.

4

Weather & Force Majeure

Tours affected by unsafe weather or conditions beyond our control will be rescheduled where possible. We are not liable for delays caused by traffic, weather, or third-party transport providers.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1

Information We Collect

When you book a tour or contact us, we collect the details you provide directly: your name, email, phone number, and any pickup or trip details needed to run your tour.

2

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to confirm and manage your booking, communicate with you about your trip, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Payment Security

Card and e-wallet payments are processed through our payment partners' own secure systems. We do not store your full card details on our servers.

Your Choices
