Cancellation and Refund Policy

Uncle Ron’s Travel and Tours understands that travel plans may change. This policy explains how customers may request cancellations, rescheduling, and refunds for tour reservations.

1

Customer-Initiated Cancellations

Customers who wish to cancel a reservation must submit a cancellation request through the booking system or contact the agency directly. The request should include the booking reference and reason for cancellation.

The applicable refund will depend on the cancellation notice period, package conditions, and any disclosed nonrefundable charges, subject to applicable law.

Proposed refund schedule for eligible refundable amounts:
  • 7 or more days before the tour: % refund
  • 3–6 days before the tour: % refund
  • 1–2 days before the tour: % refund
  • Less than 24 hours before the tour or no-show: No refund may apply where clearly disclosed and legally permitted.
2

Agency-Initiated Cancellations

If the agency cancels a tour and cannot provide an acceptable alternative, customers will be offered the applicable refund or other legally required remedy.

3

Weather and Safety Concerns

Tours may be postponed, modified, or canceled because of severe weather, government restrictions, transportation disruptions, or safety concerns. Customers will be informed of available rescheduling or refund options in accordance with applicable law and the disclosed package conditions.

4

Rescheduling Requests

Customers may request a change of travel date, subject to availability, supplier conditions, and any applicable charges disclosed before the change is confirmed.

5

Refund Processing

All refund requests are subject to review and verification. Once approved, the agency will inform the customer of the refund amount, method, and expected processing period.

Actual processing time may depend on the payment provider or financial institution. A refund will only be marked as completed after the return of funds has been verified.

6

How to Request a Cancellation or Refund

Please provide your booking reference, registered name, reason for cancellation, and relevant supporting information through the booking system or official customer service channel.

7

Important Notice

The agency will handle cancellations and refund requests fairly and in accordance with applicable Philippine consumer protection laws. This policy does not exclude any rights or remedies that customers are legally entitled to receive.
